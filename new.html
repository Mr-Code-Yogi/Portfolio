About

I am an early-career AI researcher and Junior Research Fellow at the AI Centre for Research, Swami Rama Himalayan University, Dehradun, working on applied Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, and interdisciplinary AI — including AI for healthcare and AI applications in Indian Knowledge Systems.

In 2026, I contributed to a system ranked 3rd out of 47 international teams at SemEval‑2026 Task 4, part of ACL 2026 in San Diego, and served as a peer reviewer for two workshop papers in narrative understanding and NLP. My publication record spans a shared-task system paper in the ACL Anthology, a Springer book chapter on AI-based Ayurvedic diagnosis, and a presented paper on multimodal AI for spiritual well‑being at IIT Mandi's Mind, Brain & Consciousness Conference.

I am committed to undergraduate and postgraduate teaching in AI, Machine Learning, and Computer Science, and I'm looking toward research and teaching opportunities — including PhD and MS programs — that let me keep working at the boundary between rigorous AI methods and interdisciplinary, real-world domains.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Five interconnected threads running through my publications, projects, and fellowships.

Natural Language Processing

Narrative similarity reasoning, semantic evaluation, entity recognition, and LLM-based prompting strategies — grounded in a SemEval‑2026 shared‑task placement (3rd/47 teams) and workshop peer review.

Knowledge Graphs

LLM-driven knowledge graph construction from unstructured documents, with source-linked evidence retrieval and interactive graph visualization — built and led as part of the HistoAI research platform.

Generative AI & RAG

Retrieval-augmented generation over domain-specific knowledge bases, hallucination mitigation through local LLM benchmarking, and structured-extraction pipelines with source verification.

Computer Vision

On-device TFLite image-quality classification, contactless fingerprint matching, and real-time liveness detection — developed for a UIDAI Open Innovation proposal and Ayurvedic diagnosis research.

AI × Indian Knowledge Systems

AI applied to Ayurveda and consciousness studies — an expert system for AI tongue diagnosis and a multimodal framework for spiritual well‑being, both presented at IIT Mandi's MBCC.

AI-Monitors at SemEval-2026 Task 4: A Hybrid Embedding and LLM Ensemble for Narrative Similarity

Gaurav Kumar et al. — Team AI-Monitors

20th International Workshop on Semantic Evaluation (SemEval-2026), ACL 2026, San Diego, USA

Structured prompting strategies for large language models applied to narrative similarity reasoning, combined into a hybrid ensemble of sentence embedding and LLM-based models. Components were selected by error diversity rather than accuracy alone, reaching 75% test accuracy — close to the human annotator ceiling of 78%. Team AI-Monitors ranked 3rd of 47 international teams; also served as peer reviewer for two workshop papers in narrative understanding and NLP.
